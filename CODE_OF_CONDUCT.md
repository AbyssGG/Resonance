# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want `Resonance` to be a respectful, technically serious, and collaborative open-source project.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- giving constructive technical feedback
- discussing disagreements in good faith
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- personal attacks, harassment, or intimidation
- abusive or insulting language
- bad-faith argumentation or deliberate derailment
- publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not align with this policy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact channel listed in `SUPPORT.md`.
